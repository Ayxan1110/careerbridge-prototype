      Work rules for students

      In Latvia, how much you may work depends on your residence permit. Check the basics before you accept an offer.

      • Signed contract before day one
      • Hours within your permit limit
      • Employer registers the job
      • Study permit holders can usually take part-time work next to their studies. Ask your university for the exact hour limit.
      • Your employer registers the job with the authorities. Never start before you have a signed contract.
      • Graduates need a new permit route to stay and work after the degree.

      Demo content only. Always confirm with your university and the Office of Citizenship and Migration Affairs (PMLP).
